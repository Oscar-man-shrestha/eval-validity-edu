\begin{abstract}
Educational next-item Recall@K can reward sticky or curriculum-ordered
transitions rather than structure learning. We preregister a confirmatory
test of whether the paired GRU$-$Markov non-repeat Recall@5 contrast flips
sign between native and matched cluster units (\(m_{\mathrm{planned}}=6\),
Bonferroni; GRU seeds 20261101/02/03; two-method intersection on
Junyi/ASSISTments). Confirmatory cells remain \texttt{pending\_confirmatory}
until a single post-registration touch-test. Exploratory ranking is reported
separately.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Aggregate next-item metrics on educational logs mix educational progression
with platform action structure. This paper contributes an evaluation-validity
protocol whose primary claim is a native~$\leftrightarrow$~cluster
GRU$-$Markov sign flip---not a recommender SOTA.

\section{Methods}
Protocol details match \texttt{docs/PREREGISTRATION.md}. Freeze seed
\texttt{20261004}; confirmatory fit on freeze train+val with frozen config and
fixed \texttt{best\_epoch}; bootstrap \(\ge 10{,}000\) sequence-level
resamples. Required models: popularity, recency, \texttt{markov\_order1},
\texttt{gru\_probe}.
